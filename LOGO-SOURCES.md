# Local SVG sources

All displayed tool and social marks use local files under `public/logos/`, rendered through `ToolLogo.tsx`. No icon CDN or new package is required.

Make, Zapier, Gemini, Airtable, Google Sheets, Google Forms, Gmail, Google Drive, Typeform, Calendly, Asana, Telegram, GitHub, and Upwork silhouettes were obtained from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), using its `develop/icons/<slug>.svg` files. Slugs are `make`, `zapier`, `googlegemini`, `airtable`, `googlesheets`, `googleforms`, `gmail`, `googledrive`, `typeform`, `calendly`, `asana`, `telegram`, `github`, and `upwork` respectively. Brand colors are applied locally. These are normalized single-color silhouettes, not multicolor brand lockups. Simple Icons publishes its icon data under CC0; brand usage remains subject to the respective brand's rules. See its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md).

LinkedIn and WhatsApp SVGs are retained from the supplied customized portfolio. Slack's multicolor SVG is retained from the supplied original template. The original template LICENSE is preserved.

`ai-by-zapier.svg` uses the Zapier mark with the adjacent text “AI by Zapier”; it does not claim to be a distinct official AI-product mark. `webhooks.svg` is a generic integration symbol created for this project, rather than a company logo. There are no missing SVGs that you need to supply manually. You may replace any file with your preferred approved official variant while retaining its filename and viewBox.
