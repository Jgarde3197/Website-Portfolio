About

Process logic meets modern automation

I'm a technically oriented professional working in AI and business workflow automation, with previous hands-on exposure to industrial automation and current practical experience building AI-assisted automation projects.

That path started on a production floor. At Epson Precision (Philippines) Inc., I supported engineering teams transitioning manual operations into automated machine processes — editing machine programs, reviewing flowcharts, and testing inputs and outputs until a system behaved the way it was supposed to. At Japan Tobacco International, I worked inside a SCADA-monitored production line, tracking material flow and reporting bottlenecks before they became downtime. That was industrial process work, not software or API development — but it built the same underlying discipline I now apply to business systems.

Today I apply that discipline to business automation with the same sequence every time: understand the process, define the inputs and outputs, map the logic, connect the applications, handle the exceptions, and test the complete workflow before calling it done. In practice, that means mapping data through Make.com, adding error-catch branches for the steps that can fail, and wiring Gemini AI into the process where a person used to make a judgment call manually.

Dental Appointment & Status Management Workflow

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manual scheduling work and inconsistently updated appointment records create administrative overhead for dental practices.

WORKFLOW

A new Calendly booking triggers the workflow, which captures the patient's appointment into a centralized Google Sheets tracker and notifies the dentist with a simple approve/decline action.

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Make.com routes the dentist's approval or rescheduling decision, then automates the matching confirmation or rescheduling email back to the patient. Status changes and cancellations are synchronized back to the tracker.

RELIABILITY / EDGE CASES

Includes workflow safeguards for temporary service or connection failures, so a dropped connection doesn't leave an appointment record out of sync.

BUSINESS VALUE

Designed to reduce administrative workload and the risk of missed appointment updates, and to give dental staff a more organized way to manage the daily appointment flow.

AI Lead Management & Sales Alert Workflow

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manual lead handling leads to delayed responses, scattered records, and a higher risk of missing high-priority prospects.

WORKFLOW

A webhook-based intake receives the lead the moment it comes in, and Gemini AI processes the prospect information to assess quality and priority.

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Make.com records structured lead information in Google Sheets, drafts a personalized follow-up email, and sends a Telegram alert for high-priority leads.

RELIABILITY / EDGE CASES

Includes retry/error-handling logic for temporary database interruptions — the workflow retries the failed step three times at 15-minute intervals before giving up.

BUSINESS VALUE

Helps minimize response delays, keeps lead records structured and centralized, and reduces the repetitive manual work of triaging incoming leads.

AI-Assisted E-Commerce Order Processing Workflow

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manual order processing creates data-entry bottlenecks, delays confirmations, and risks missing special delivery instructions.

WORKFLOW

Incoming customer orders are recorded automatically, and AI classifies the delivery instructions attached to each order.

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Make.com generates an automated confirmation email and applies conditional logic to flag high-value orders for priority review.

RELIABILITY / EDGE CASES

Handles invalid customer email addresses without stopping the main workflow, isolating that fault so the rest of the run completes.

BUSINESS VALUE

Designed to reduce manual data entry, keep delivery instructions from getting missed, and give faster visibility into priority orders.

AI Receipt & Expense Data Extraction Workflow

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manually typing expense details from receipt images into spreadsheets is slow and error-prone.

WORKFLOW

Receipt attachments arriving by email are picked up automatically, and Gemini Vision AI extracts the date, vendor, line items, and totals.

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Make.com structures the extracted information and records it in Google Sheets, applying validation filters against the extracted fields.

RELIABILITY / EDGE CASES

Includes reconnect/error-handling logic and validation that prevents invalid or junk entries from entering the workflow where possible.

BUSINESS VALUE

Reduces manual entry from receipts and helps keep the expense log limited to validated, structured records.
