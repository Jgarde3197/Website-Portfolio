About

Process logic meets modern automation

I'm a technically oriented professional transitioning into AI and business workflow automation, with previous hands-on exposure to industrial automation and current practical experience building AI automation projects.

That path started on a production floor. At Epson Precision (Philippines) Inc., I supported engineering teams transitioning manual operations into automated machine processes — editing machine programs, reviewing flowcharts, and testing inputs and outputs until a system behaved the way it was supposed to. At Japan Tobacco International, I worked inside a SCADA-monitored production line, tracking material flow and reporting bottlenecks before they became downtime.

Underneath both roles is the same discipline: input, process, output. Trace where data comes from, understand what happens to it in between, and confirm what comes out the other side. That's exactly what I now apply to Make.com scenarios — mapping data, adding error-catch branches, and wiring Gemini AI into the process step so the automation does the judgment work a person used to do manually.

Automated expense tracking & Vision AI receipt processing

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manually typing expense details from receipt images into spreadsheets is slow and error-prone.

AUTOMATION SOLUTION

Inspects incoming receipt attachments, extracts structured line items with Vision AI, validates each file against an is_receipt gatekeeper filter, and logs structured expense records — with a retry handler for failed extractions.

Dental appointment automation & status management system

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Manual scheduling work and inconsistently updated appointment records create administrative overhead for dental practices.

AUTOMATION SOLUTION

Automatically captures new patient appointments into a centralized tracker, notifies the dentist of new bookings with a simple approve/decline flow, sends patients the appropriate confirmation or rescheduling message based on that decision, and keeps appointment statuses synced when patients cancel — immediately alerting the dental team that a slot has opened. Built-in reliability safeguards keep appointments, updates, and notifications processing through temporary service or connection issues.

BUSINESS VALUE

Reduces administrative workload, minimizes missed appointment updates, improves response time to patients, and gives dental staff a more organized and reliable way to manage their daily appointment flow.
